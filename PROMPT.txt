This is a repository for:
- a 2d platformer speedrunning game called Fell Runner
- physics and gameplay like celeste
- 3 levels based on the most notorious lake district fells
- 3 running vest choices
- seeded random weather
- 3 difficulty choices which dictate weather severity
    - none
    - normal
    - extreme
- wind affects player physics
- about a minute per level
- best times and ghosts recorded
- offer donations to https://penrithmrt.org.uk/
- maximise accessibility
